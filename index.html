03 / THE CREATOR'S JOURNEY

BUILT FROM A DREAM

ONE CREATOR.
ONE BIG DREAM.

A personal project by an independent Japanese student developer.

FOLLOW ON X ↗ WATCH ON YOUTUBE ↗

I'm a Japanese student who has always loved not only playing games, but also making them, drawing, and bringing new ideas to life.

One day, I created a simple game on another platform. Seeing players enjoy something I had made meant a lot to me. That experience inspired me to pursue my dream of becoming a game creator.

As my ambitions grew, I wanted to make games with higher quality and more depth, so I decided to take on Roblox development. Writing code felt difficult at first, and for a long time I hesitated to begin. Eventually, I challenged myself to take the next step: building a tower defense game on my own.

Tower defense has been one of my favorite genres for a long time. Drawing on what I learned as a player, I started asking myself, “Wouldn't it be interesting if a tower defense game had this feature?” I turned those ideas into systems and features that I wanted to experience myself.

Origin Recode: Second World is still a work in progress. It has not yet reached the vision I have in mind, but I will keep updating, learning, and improving it until it becomes closer to the game I dream of making.

I'm building this world one step at a time, with the goal of becoming the best game creator I can be. Thank you for being part of the journey.
