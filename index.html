05 / THE CREATOR
ONE CREATOR
ONE BIG DREAM

BUILT FROM A DREAM

One Creator.
One Big Dream.

I am a student from Japan who has always loved not only playing games, but also making them, drawing, and creating things from scratch.

I once made a small game on another platform, and seeing people play something I had created made me incredibly happy. That experience inspired me to pursue my dream of becoming a game creator.

I wanted to build higher-quality games, so I decided to develop on Roblox. Coding was difficult, and getting started was not easy. Still, I took the next step and began building a tower defense game I truly love—all by myself.

I kept asking myself, “Wouldn’t it be cool if a tower defense game had this feature?” Those ideas became systems such as the Gold Market, NPC interactions, a skill tree, and unit Mastery—features designed to take the experience beyond traditional tower defense.

ORIGIN RECODE: SECOND WORLD is still a work in progress, and it has not yet reached the vision I have for it. But with every update, I hope to bring it closer to the game I have always dreamed of making.

I am one creator with one big dream.
And I will keep working toward becoming the best game creator I can be.
